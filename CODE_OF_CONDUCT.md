# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (abridged)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Steroids a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, be precise, assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, trolling, insults, or unwelcome attention — in issues,
  PRs, chats, or any project space.
- Reports carry evidence; the human reading them is owed the same respect
  as the code under review.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at
**TBC** — enforcement contact pending (human ask outstanding).
Maintainers will review, investigate, and respond, and may remove, edit,
or reject contributions not aligned with this Code. Until the contact is
staffed, report via any channel that reaches a maintainer; reports are
queued, not dropped.
